Quick answers

Can they send Interac e-Transfers?Not in the U.S. They can link the U.S. account to PayPal to pay people.
Is the money insured?FDIC up to $250,000 per ownership category. U.S. deposits aren’t CDIC insured.
Do they need a U.S. address?No. They apply with their Canadian address, and the card uses Canadian credit history.
What about ATM fees?50,000+ no-fee ATMs. Others may charge, and the fee is shown before they accept.
What after year one?Direct Checking is US$39.50 a year with eStatements, US$59.50 with paper. The card stays $0.
Spending outside the U.S.?Signature Black charges 1.5% on non-USD purchases. Black Plus charges none.
